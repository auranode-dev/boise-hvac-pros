Finding a trustworthy HVAC contractor isn't easy. Idaho HVAC Licensing Facts

In Idaho, HVAC work is regulated at the state level. Here's what you should know about contractor licensing to protect yourself as a homeowner:

  • All HVAC contractors must be licensed through the Idaho Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses (DOPL)
  • An HVAC contractor must have worked as a licensed journeyman for at least 24 months before obtaining a contractor license
  • HVAC journeymen must complete an approved apprenticeship program (576 hours of instruction) plus 8,000 hours of supervised work experience
  • Contractors must pass the Idaho HVAC contractor's exam and pay the required fees
  • A $2,000 compliance bond is required for the duration of the license period
  • Any technician handling refrigerants must hold EPA Section 608 certification (a federal requirement independent of state licensing)
  • Mechanical permits are required for HVAC installation, and work must be inspected

Always ask to see a contractor's license and verify it through the DOPL website before authorizing any work. This protects you, ensures work meets code requirements, and keeps your warranty valid.
