Common HVAC Questions from Boise Homeowners

How long do AC units last in Boise? Central air conditioners typically last 12 to 15 years. In Boise's hot, dry climate, regular maintenance is key to reaching the upper end of that range. Annual tune-ups and filter changes can extend system life significantly.

When should I replace my furnace? Furnaces generally last 15 to 25 years. If your furnace is over 15 years old and needs frequent repairs, or if your energy bills are climbing, it may be time to consider a replacement. A professional inspection can help you decide.

Do I need a license to do HVAC work in Idaho? Yes. Idaho law requires HVAC contractors to be licensed through DOPL. This includes completing an approved apprenticeship or work-experience pathway, passing an exam, and maintaining a $2,000 compliance bond. Always verify your contractor's license before hiring.
