Seasonal HVAC Prep Guide for Boise Homeowners

Boise has two demanding seasons for your HVAC system. Hot summers stress your AC. Long winters stress your furnace. Do the right maintenance at the right time to keep both reliable.

Spring (March – May) — Prepare Your AC

Boise's heat arrives fast. Spring is the time to prepare your cooling system before the first 100-degree day.

  • Schedule a spring AC tune-up in March or April, before demand spikes.
  • Replace the air filter.
  • Clear the area around your outdoor condenser.
  • Check for ice or debris on the refrigerant lines.
  • Test the system on a mild day.

Learn more on our AC repair and maintenance pages.

Summer (June – August) — Keep Your AC Running

During peak cooling season, small problems become emergencies fast.

  • Check and replace the filter every 1 to 2 months.
  • Keep the thermostat at a steady setting.
  • Keep blinds closed during the hottest part of the day.
  • Watch for weak airflow or warm air, which are early signs of a refrigerant issue.
  • If the AC struggles on the hottest days, schedule service before a breakdown.

Fall (September – November) — Prepare Your Furnace

Idaho winters bring freezing temperatures. Fall is the critical window to make sure your heating system is ready.

  • Schedule a fall furnace tune-up in September or October.
  • Test the furnace before nights turn freezing.
  • Replace the air filter for heating season.
  • Check that vents and registers are open and unblocked.
  • Verify your carbon monoxide detector has fresh batteries. See our furnace repair page.

Winter (December – February) — Keep Your Furnace Running

During peak heating season, a furnace failure in freezing weather is an emergency.

  • Replace the filter every 1 to 2 months.
  • Keep snow and ice clear of the furnace exhaust and intake vents.
  • Set the thermostat consistently.
  • If the furnace cycles oddly or blows cold air, call a technician early.
  • Never ignore a yellow flame or a CO detector alarm. Turn off the furnace, ventilate, evacuate, and call a professional.

Year-Round Rule: Two Tune-Ups a Year

Plan two professional visits: a spring AC tune-up and a fall furnace tune-up. Boise's climate demands both. Annual maintenance prevents costly failures and keeps energy bills low.
